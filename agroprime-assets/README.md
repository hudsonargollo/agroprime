## Audiences

Investors ("socios inversionistas") weighing a cattle-financing proposition; producers looking for genetics and feed; retail buyers of premium cuts; local hires (marketing, butchers, feed-mill staff) — recruitment is currently the loudest content stream on IG/FB.

## Brand look

- **Logo:** circular gold badge — charging bull inside a laurel wreath, farmhouse and rising sun behind it, ribbon "Negociaciones y Servicios Ganaderos / Anez Hurtado". Reads heritage, not startup.
- **Palette (sampled):** black `#0B0B0B`, deep olive-green `#1C2410`/`#384818`, gold `#E8B050` with highlight `#F8D860` and bronze shadow `#704010`, warm cream `#F5EFE0`, crimson `#A00000` for Carnes Prime.
- **Type feel:** heavy gold serif/display for headlines (all-caps), clean sans for body. On social, heavy black panels with gold rules and thin gold borders.
- **Photography:** white Nelore/Brahman cattle, feedlot alleys, golden-hour pasture, premium cut close-ups; some AI-generated hero imagery (cowgirl, "Mujer Boliviana").

## Content pattern

About half the feed is hiring ads; the rest is product/genetics proof, a member benefit, an anniversary greeting and a charity post. Engagement is very low (0–25 likes, 9 IG followers), so the website has to carry credibility the socials don't: real facilities, animal records, named genetics, team, and clear investor terms. Tone is formal Spanish with emoji accents, Bolivian "usted" in corporate copy and "vos" in retail copy.

## Suggested website spine

Home (hero statement + four pillars + CTA "Quiero invertir") · Genética · Confinamiento y alimentación · Negociaciones · Grupo y socios (benefits + Carnes Prime) · Nosotros (Anez Hurtado, Santa Cruz) · Contacto (three phone lines, WhatsApp, office map) · a discreet "Trabajá con nosotros" page for the rrhh recruitment flow.
